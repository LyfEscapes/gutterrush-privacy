Gutter Rush

Privacy Policy · LyfEscapes LLC · Effective November 7, 2026

Gutter Rush is a game by LyfEscapes LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Gutter Rush on Android.

What we collect

Nothing. Gutter Rush has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics of our own and no servers. We never see your name, email, contacts, photos, files or location.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, caps, upgrades, skins, best times, sound settings and which items you own are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Gutter Rush shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the world map, an occasional full-screen ad between stages, and optional rewarded ads (to continue, get an extra life or double your caps). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about your device, how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data. Google collects and processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before any ad is shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the shop ("Ad privacy choices"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, or Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads).

If you buy "Remove Ads" or the "Supporter Bundle", the app no longer shows ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details. The app only learns which items you own so it can unlock them.

Children

Gutter Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Data sent by the Google services above travels encrypted (HTTPS).

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google, use the privacy controls in your Google Account and phone settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
